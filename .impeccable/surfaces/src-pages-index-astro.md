## Direction contract
THESIS: The portfolio is a product launch keynote. Each project gets its moment on stage, chapter by chapter, and hublii is the finale ("one more thing"). It refuses the category default: a grid of project cards with a hero tagline.
OWN-WORLD: The default is light mode, a bright keynote-slide stage: white/near-white ground with tight, heavy Archivo display type (wide stretch) and JetBrains Mono presenter-cue labels. Colour comes from STAGE LIGHTING GELS. Each chapter washes the stage in its project's own light: hublii moss #5E7A4E + amber #D9A441 on cream, FirstLine blue #2563EB + cyan #06B6D4, MasHoorCake mint #9BD3C7 + blush #F3D3D8, Bethesda sun #F5B041 + chocolate #4A2C1E, Pawmetric lavender #A98FE0 + gold #E9B949. The house accent is stage amber. Dark mode ("house lights down") is a black stage with a soft, feathered, volumetric spotlight that follows the pointer (lerped, dust shimmer, never hard-edged), with a slight blur on everything outside the light.
STORY: A visitor learns Hamid is an inventor whose ideas come to life. They see the work rise through a podium (#3 MasHoorCake, #2 FirstLine, #1 hublii), understand hublii in depth and that it is on a live waitlist, see what's in development, then the encore projects, the backstage story and goals, and a quiet curtain-call contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Light stage. A presenter bar on top: name, chapter counter and ticks, house-lights toggle, and a sound pill with a hover volume slider. Centre: a spotlight pool lands on Hamid's portrait. "Hamid Javaheri." is set huge beneath it, with the inventor line under that and a run-of-show strip at the bottom. Scroll is the only call to action.
FORM: Launch Keynote (the pick card on the hand; 1st on my ordered list), with the user's raises: per-project colour gels; keynote-style exploded architecture diagrams that assemble on scroll, borrowed from the Build Book idea (FirstLine = web and native apps joined by a backend bridge; hublii = a stack of layers) and always labelled in plain words; and rich, real project screenshots. Seed key b4800e91.
SIGNATURE: Pinned, scrubbed chapters. The podium blocks rise. "One more thing…" clears into the hublii reveal. The exploded diagrams click together as you scroll.
AUDIO: Ambient music at 5% volume. It tries to autoplay; if the browser blocks it, it starts on the first user activation (pointerdown, keydown, touch). A visible sound pill with a hover/focus volume slider; mute is remembered.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
